Live dashboard

flutter_chartjs_port · Supabase Realtime

A line chart of a Supabase table. Each browser gets an anonymous session: it adds two rows a second of its own, and it sees a shared 6-hour history and its own rows only, live through Realtime. Drag, scroll or swipe the chart to go back in time: the older rows load one page at a time.
