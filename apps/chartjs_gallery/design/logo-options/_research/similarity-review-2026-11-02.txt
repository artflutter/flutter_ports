Flutter Ports shortlist: preliminary visual similarity review
Checked: 2026-11-02

Scope
Reviewed Kinetic Bloom (15, 15B), Type Play (17, 17B), and their combination
(18) using descriptive web/image searches and direct visual inspection of
downloaded public reference artwork. This was NOT a reverse-image upload
search or an exhaustive trademark/register search. It cannot identify the
image generator's training influences or prove originality or copying.

Conclusion
No exact match was found in the reference artwork actually inspected.
Both ideas have existing visual relatives. Neither is established as unique.
The full Type Play wordmark is more differentiated in this sample than its
standalone tt avatar. Bloom needs a more specific silhouette before selection.
These are design judgments, not findings of infringement or legal clearance.

Bloom comparisons
1. CJ Group Blossom: three separate soft colored petals. Related number of
   parts, organic shapes and multicolor arrangement. CJ's petals are fuller
   teardrops without our inward-curving rotating blades. Not an exact match.
   Official identity: https://www.cj.net/kr/en/cj-introduction
   Official logo library: https://newsroom.cj.net/medialibrary/cj-logos/
   Inspected image: https://framerusercontent.com/images/G9QWIiCvXor6iDj9sihNlCpniOc.png?height=486&width=798
   That image was indexed against NdotLight, but it visibly depicts CJ, not
   NdotLight's own brand. Do not propagate the image-search description.

2. Youth of Peel: three rotating curved segments, with red and two blues.
   Related radial structure, concave inner edges and color distribution.
   Its segments are pointed, form a tighter triangular perimeter, and surround
   a larger round center; ours are rounder and more separated. Not identical.
   https://youthofpeel.ca/
   Inspected image: https://youthofpeel.ca/assets/logo-icon-CYE5-yKF.png

3. Image indexed as IDP Education: three leaf-shaped orange/green/blue parts
   around a circular void. Broad three-part organic motif, different geometry.
   Inspected image: https://cdncontent.halotechnologies.com.au/images/LOGOS-CORPORATE/mark_light/iel-au.png
   This reference image came from a third-party market-data provider; do not
   treat its identity or currency as independently established by this review.
   Related first-party design discussion: https://www.ddg.com.tw/en/works/idp-education/

4. Oxford Said Business School climate challenge windmill graphic: three
   semicircles rotated around a point, orange on yellow. Demonstrates that
   the pinwheel motif is common, but is not a close match in contour or color.
   https://www.sbs.ox.ac.uk/climate-change-challenge/climate-change-challenge-2023
   Inspected image: https://www.sbs.ox.ac.uk/sites/default/files/CCCIcon12windmill.png

Type Play comparisons
1. TT Seoul: heavy lowercase tt, two upright stems, rightward feet, horizontal
   crossbars. Shares the basic standalone letter pair. Its letters are separate,
   with flat horizontal bars and squared feet; our bar is joined, blue and
   rising, and our lower terminals are curved. Not an exact match.
   https://www.ttseoul.com/news
   Inspected image: https://framerusercontent.com/images/PYAOtLdyjNb9alSMWofOjzO5v4.png?height=1080&width=1080

2. TaskTango: a pair of tilted uppercase T shapes. Related double-T idea and
   diagonal alignment; different case, endpoints and crossbar construction.
   https://nordiclabs.dev/
   Inspected image: https://nordiclabs.dev/TaskTango_Logo.png
   The image was retrievable; the site's text was unavailable through web open.

3. Sabuj Ali TT Monogram: mirrored uppercase outlined serif-like T forms.
   A generic conceptual relative, not a close visual match on inspection.
   https://dribbble.com/shots/20402231-TT-Monogram
   Inspected image: https://cdn.dribbble.com/userupload/4290386/file/original-90fa9b93f619af5b72e9dfc326eba006.jpg?compress=1&resize=1200x900

4. Tratos: serif uppercase T plus smaller lowercase t, one blue color.
   A search hit rejected as a close match after inspecting the artwork.
   https://x.com/TratosGroup
   Inspected image: https://pbs.twimg.com/profile_images/884348443753316353/7xjhlMvD.jpg

Limitations and excluded evidence
Some stock-logo listings were inaccessible. A downloaded VectorStock image
was an access-denied notice, not logo artwork, and was excluded. A TravelTime
image URL returned 404 and was excluded. Search-generated image descriptions
were not sufficient evidence of shape or attribution.

Design recommendation
Keep the complete flutter ports wordmark as the stronger candidate for further
development. Do not treat the tt avatar as a distinctive identity by itself.
Redraw Bloom with a brand-specific silhouette if retaining it; recoloring a
three-petal pinwheel alone does not establish a distinctive mark. Review final
vector artwork again before adopting it. Preserve existing files as drafts.
