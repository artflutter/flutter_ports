Use case: logo-brand. Completely fresh branding exploration for exact brand name "Flutter Ports", an independent initiative translating great JavaScript libraries into native Dart/Flutter. Direction 19 HERMIT. A witty, beautifully designed minimalist hermit crab mascot: a compact little crab carrying an angular cobalt shell, two curious eyes and two simple coral claws, strong graphic silhouette. Idea: useful libraries finding a new native home. The crab must look like an intentional professional brand mark, not clipart, emoji, children’s cartoon, or a detailed illustration. Use 5-7 bold flat shapes total, character from posture and proportions, no smile or face on the shell, no thin legs. Asymmetrical angular shell, soft claws, appealing visual tension. Navy, cobalt and burnt coral, warm ivory background. A distinctive editorial serif wordmark "Flutter Ports" next to the icon, medium weight, excellent letter spacing; avoid the generic rounded sans used by tech startups. No doorway, portal, elliptical ring, pinwheel, petals, FP monogram, TT ligature, spark, cube, Flutter official mark or Microsoft Office imagery. One landscape identity board: small heading "19 / HERMIT", one large horizontal lockup, below a small black-only faithful lockup, a reverse white on navy version, and a tiny mascot avatar. Make the icon work in one color. No gradients, gloss, 3D, mockup objects, drop shadows, slogans or textures. This must be a new creative territory with an appealing mascot personality.
