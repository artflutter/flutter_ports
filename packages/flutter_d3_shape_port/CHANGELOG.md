## 0.1.0

- Initial release: native Dart port of `d3-shape` v3.2.0 (`arc`, `pie`, `line`, `area`,
  `lineRadial`, `areaRadial`, `pointRadial`, `link`, `linkHorizontal`, `linkVertical`,
  `linkRadial`, `symbol`, `symbolsFill`, `symbolsStroke`, all 13 built-in symbol types, `stack`,
  all 5 stack offsets, all 6 stack orders, and all 18 curves), with direct Flutter `Canvas`
  rendering via `UiPathContext` from `flutter_d3_path_port`.
