## 0.1.0

- Initial release: native, fully statically typed pure-Dart port of `d3-quadtree` v3.0.1
  (`quadtree`, `quadtreeBy`, `Quadtree<T>`, sealed `QuadtreeNode<T>` / `QuadtreeInternalNode<T>` /
  `QuadtreeLeafNode<T>`, `x`/`y` accessors, `extent`/`setExtent`, `cover`, `add`/`addAll`,
  `remove`/`removeAll`, `copy`, `root`, `data`, `size`, `find`, `visit`, `visitAfter`, and
  `d3QuadtreeVersion`).
