# Chart.js ADR 0015: Text

- Status: accepted (2026-10-21)
- Date: 2026-10-19
- Related: [Chart.js ADR 0013](0013-canvas-model.md) (the canvas layer),
  [Chart.js ADR 0009](0009-no-display-only-js-exactness.md) (no display-only exactness),
  [Chart.js ADR 0011](0011-third-party-sources.md) (third-party sources),
  [porting-flow.md](../porting-flow.md) §2.8 and §3, [text-metrics.md](../text-metrics.md)

## Context

Chart.js sets `ctx.font` to the CSS shorthand that `toFontString` makes
(`helpers.canvas.ts:25-34`): `[style] [weight] <size>px <family list>`. The default family list is
`"'Helvetica Neue', 'Helvetica', 'Arial', sans-serif"` (`core.defaults.js:55`). Chart.js reads
`measureText(text).width` for the layout of ticks, legends, titles and tooltips. It reads the
`actualBoundingBox*` values only for the underline and the strikethrough of `renderText`
(`helpers.canvas.ts:422-426`).

`toBeCloseToPixel` (0.5 % or less than 2 px) occurs 374 times in 16 upstream spec files. These
specs measure real fonts. Upstream runs them in Chrome on Ubuntu. There, the default family list
resolves to Liberation Sans, a font with the metrics of Arial and Helvetica.

`dart:ui` gives `ui.ParagraphBuilder`, `ui.TextStyle` and `ui.Paragraph`. A paragraph gives the
advance width, the alphabetic and ideographic baselines, and the line metrics. It gives no glyph
ink boxes and no OS/2 typographic metrics. The flutter tester resolves each family that no test
registered to its square FlutterTest font, with each glyph one em wide. A text style with no
family also gets that font. Registered fonts cannot replace it.

## Decision

### 1. Font parser

The `font` setter of `UiCanvasContext` parses the CSS shorthand with `parseCanvasFont`
(`lib/src/canvas/canvas_ui_text.dart`) into a `CanvasFont`:
- The tokens before the size are keywords. `normal`, `small-caps` and the stretch keywords have
  no effect. `italic` and `oblique` give the italic style. `bold` and `bolder` give weight 700.
  `lighter` gives weight 100. A number from 1 to 1000 gives the nearest `ui.FontWeight`. Another
  token makes the font invalid.
- The size is a number with the unit `px` or `pt`. The parser converts `pt` to `px` (4/3). It
  reads and drops a line height after `/`, because canvas text ignores it.
- The family list follows the size. It has names in quotes or without quotes, separated by commas.
  It ends at the first generic family (`serif`, `sans-serif`, `monospace` and the other CSS
  generic names). The first family is the `fontFamily` of the `ui.TextStyle`. The other families
  are the `fontFamilyFallback`.
- A generic family stays a family name. `sans-serif` is the family name `'sans-serif'`, not "no
  family". On a device and on the web, an unknown family name falls back to the platform default.
  Thus the visible result is the platform default. In the flutter tester, the harness registers
  the test font under `sans-serif` (section 4).
- A font that does not parse is `null`. The setter then ignores the value, as the DOM does. The
  default font is `10px sans-serif`.
- The `font` getter gives the string as the code set it. Chromium normalizes it. No Chart.js source
  reads `ctx.font`.
- The parser caches its results, with a bound of 256 entries. A full cache starts again empty.

### 2. Paragraphs and caches

- `fillText`, `strokeText` and `measureText` lay out one `ui.Paragraph` for each font and text.
  `prepareCanvasText` first changes each ASCII whitespace character to a space, because canvas
  text is one line.
- `layoutCanvasText` keeps an LRU cache of 512 paragraphs. The key is the font, the text, the
  direction and the paint: the final color (with `globalAlpha`) for a fill, and the color and the
  stroke values for a stroke. A gradient or a pattern gives a paragraph without a cache, with a
  foreground shader that maps back to user space. The context disposes that paragraph after the
  draw.
- The cache disposes the paragraph that it evicts, as `TextPainter` disposes its old paragraph. A
  recorded picture keeps its own reference to the text that a paragraph painted. Left to the
  garbage collector, a text that changes in each frame piles up one native paragraph for each
  frame.
- `measureCanvasText` keeps an LRU cache of 2048 metrics entries, keyed by the font and the text.
  A paragraph for measurement only is disposed at once.
- Text ignores `globalCompositeOperation`.
- When the code gives `maxWidth` and the advance width is larger, the layer scales x by
  `maxWidth / width`, as the DOM does. A `maxWidth` of 0, a negative value or NaN draws nothing.
- When the transform only translates, text draws with no canvas transform.

### 3. Alignment, baselines and metrics

- `textAlign` takes `start`, `end`, `left`, `right` and `center`. `start` and `end` resolve with
  `direction`.
- `textBaseline` follows `GetFontBaseline` of Chromium. `top`, `middle` and `bottom` lie on an em
  box: the paragraph ascent and descent, scaled to sum to the font size. `hanging` is at 80 % of
  the ascent. `ideographic` is the ideographic baseline of the paragraph. `alphabetic` is 0.
  `dart:ui` does not give the OS/2 typographic metrics that Chromium uses for the em box.
- `measureText` gives the advance width as `width`. `actualBoundingBoxLeft` and
  `actualBoundingBoxRight` come from the advance box at the `textAlign` anchor.
  `actualBoundingBoxAscent` and `actualBoundingBoxDescent` are the font ascent and descent,
  moved by the `textBaseline`. The signs follow the DOM. The values agree with `textAlign` and
  `textBaseline`. The layer does not implement `fontBoundingBoxAscent` and
  `fontBoundingBoxDescent`, because no Chart.js source reads them.
- Exact glyph ink boxes are not required
  ([Chart.js ADR 0009](0009-no-display-only-js-exactness.md)).
- Text under a rotation draws with its rotation snapped to steps of 2 degrees. The text point
  keeps its exact bitmap position. Only the rotation around it changes, by at most 1 degree.
  Skia (skwasm, CanvasKit) and Impeller cache the glyphs of a text for each exact rotation (a
  strike). Labels that turn in each frame ask for new strikes in each frame. On macOS, 200 such
  labels took 85 ms of raster time. On skwasm, the strike cache then churns, and the main thread
  (paragraph shaping) and the render worker (text drawing) race in
  `SkStrikeCache::findOrCreateStrike`. The page crashed with `memory access out of bounds`. A
  plain Flutter app without port code crashed the same way. Flutter `master` fixes the race
  (flutter/flutter#190048, #191014 and #194139), but no stable release has the fixes yet. The
  snap stays until the toolchain pin has them. The snap is display only
  ([Chart.js ADR 0009](0009-no-display-only-js-exactness.md)). It is in
  `UiCanvasContext._snappedTextMatrix`.

### 4. Test font

The harness installs Liberation Sans 2.1.5 (SIL OFL 1.1). It is metric-compatible with Arial and
Helvetica, and it is the font that Chrome on the upstream Ubuntu CI resolves the default family
list to.
- The files are the regular, bold, italic and bold italic TTFs of the official release
  `liberation-fonts-ttf-2.1.5.tar.gz` (SHA-256
  `7191c669bf38899f73a2094ed00f7b800553364f90e2637010a69c0e268f25d0`), from
  `https://github.com/liberationfonts/liberation-fonts/releases`. They are unchanged, with the
  `LICENSE` of the release, in `packages/chartjs_test_utils/lib/fonts/`.
- `installTestFont()` of `package:chartjs_test_utils/flutter.dart` registers the same bytes with
  `FontLoader` under `Helvetica Neue`, `Helvetica`, `Arial`, `Liberation Sans` and `sans-serif`,
  one time for each isolate. It finds `lib/fonts/` through `.dart_tool/package_config.json`,
  because `Isolate.resolvePackageUri` throws an `UnsupportedError` in the flutter tester.
- `packages/flutter_chartjs_port/test/flutter_test_config.dart` (the port of `test/index.js`) calls
  `installTestFont()` before each test file. The hunk has the `differences.md` row
  `harness-test-font`.
- Licensing (AGENTS.md Licensing): the OFL notice is a separate component of
  `packages/chartjs_test_utils/LICENSE`. `LICENSES/OFL-1.1.txt` has the license text. The last
  table of `REUSE.toml` annotates `packages/chartjs_test_utils/lib/fonts/**`. The harness is
  `publish_to: none`. Thus no published archive contains the font.

### 5. Mock context

`MockContext` of the harness replaces `fillText` and `measureText` for the specs that check
canvas calls. Its `measureText` gives the values of `src/context.js:119-135` (10 px for each
character).

### 6. Text-metric probe

`test/port/canvas/text_metrics_test.dart` compares `UiCanvasContext.measureText` with the Chrome
canvas, with the same font files. `tool/reference/text_metrics/` generates the Chrome reference.
[text-metrics.md](../text-metrics.md) records the result and the steps to run the probe again.
The test fails when a width is outside `toBeCloseToPixel`, when a baseline offset is more than
1 px from Chrome, or when the Chrome ink box is more than 0.5 px outside the box of the canvas
layer.

## Alternatives considered

- **`sans-serif` as "no family"** (the first proposal). Rejected. In the flutter tester, a style
  with no family always gets the square FlutterTest font. Registered fonts do not change that.
  A font string such as `12px sans-serif` would then measure with the square font.
- **A canvas-only text engine with OS/2 metrics.** Rejected. It parses font files for baseline
  offsets that only move pixels ([Chart.js ADR 0009](0009-no-display-only-js-exactness.md)).
- **Arial or Helvetica as the test font.** Rejected. Their licenses do not let the repository
  include them.
- **Fonts from another application bundle.** Rejected. Their source and license are not clear.
- **Looser matchers for text specs.** Rejected. A difference becomes a `differences.md` row
  ([porting-flow.md](../porting-flow.md) §7, risk 1). The probe found no width difference.

## Consequences

- Specs that measure text measure Liberation Sans in `flutter test`, as upstream measures it in
  Chrome on Ubuntu. `toBeCloseToPixel` holds for every width of the probe.
- Text widths are the only metric that controls the layout. The baseline offsets and the bounding
  boxes only move pixels. The port keeps their differences
  ([Chart.js ADR 0009](0009-no-display-only-js-exactness.md)).
- An underline of `renderText` uses `actualBoundingBoxDescent`. Thus the canvas layer draws it
  lower than Chrome: for text with no descender, 2.8 px lower at 12px. The underline and the
  strikethrough span the advance box, not the ink box.
- Specs that use a font with no registered family measure with the square FlutterTest font. The
  harness registers each family of the Chart.js default font and `sans-serif`.
- `strokeText` makes a new `Paint` for each call, also when the paragraph cache has the
  paragraph. Chart.js core calls `strokeText` rarely.

## Evidence

From [text-metrics.md](../text-metrics.md) (2026-10-19, Google Chrome 155.0.8059.80 headless and
Flutter 3.44.9 on macOS 26.5.1 arm64, the same Liberation Sans 2.1.5 files on both sides):
- The probe measured 5 fonts (the Chart.js default family list at normal 12px, bold 12px, bold
  16px, 10px and 24px) and 16 texts (tick values, labels, titles, `WWWWWWWWWW`, `iiiiiiiiii`,
  U+1E80 and a Hebrew word), 80 cases in all.
- Widths: all 80 are equal. The largest difference is 5.0e-7 px (7.9e-6 %). This is the rounding
  of the Chrome values to 6 decimals.
- Baseline offsets: each `textBaseline` is less than 1 px from Chrome. The largest difference is
  0.701 px, for `top`, `middle` and `bottom` in bold 16px. `alphabetic` has no difference. Chrome
  takes its em box from the OS/2 typographic ascender and descender (1491 and 431 font units). The
  canvas layer scales the paragraph ascent and descent, which include half of the line gap.
- Bounding boxes: the signs agree. The box of the canvas layer holds the Chrome ink box, except
  that the Chrome descent is up to 0.291 px larger (`January` in 10px).
- The Chrome page checked each width against a family name that only the page defines. Thus the
  family list resolved to the loaded files and not to the system Helvetica Neue.

From the harness tests:
- `packages/chartjs_test_utils/test/fonts_test.dart` (8 tests): `Hello` at 12px is
  4667 / 2048 x 12 px (regular) and 5007 / 2048 x 12 px (bold) for each registered family. The
  kerning of `Wo` applies. A style with no family keeps the FlutterTest font.
- `test/port/flutter_test_config_test.dart` (5 tests): Arial, Helvetica and Helvetica Neue
  measure with Liberation Sans.
- `test/port/canvas/canvas_ui_test.dart` covers the font parse cases, the `textAlign` and
  `textBaseline` ink positions, the `measureText` signs, the `maxWidth` scale, `strokeText` and
  gradient text. The group "a gradient or a pattern is in user space" checks that a gradient or
  pattern text keeps its shader in user space at identity, under a translation and with a
  `maxWidth`. The test "a disposed paragraph still paints in the recording" evicts and disposes
  a cached paragraph and disposes a gradient paragraph, and the recorded pictures still paint
  both texts.

## Open

- The probe ran on macOS arm64 only. Chrome on Linux can use other font hinting and subpixel
  settings. A run of `text_metrics_reference.mjs` on Ubuntu (for example in CI, with
  `CHROME=google-chrome`) is not done.
- The probe measures the canvas layer only in `flutter test` on the host. It does not measure the
  web renderers (CanvasKit, skwasm) or a device.
- The probe does not measure the italic faces or fallback fonts. The italic faces of Liberation
  Sans have the same advances as the upright faces, so `fonts_test.dart` cannot prove that the
  engine picks the italic face.
