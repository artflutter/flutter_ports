## 0.1.0

- Initial release: native, fully statically typed Dart port of `d3-chord` v3.0.1
  (`chord`, `chordDirected`, `chordTranspose`, `ChordLayout`, `Chords`, `Chord`,
  `ChordSubgroup`, `ChordGroup`, `ribbon`, `ribbonArrow`, `ribbonBy`,
  `ribbonArrowBy`, and `RibbonGenerator<T, S>`), with pure Dart SVG path output
  and direct Flutter `Canvas` rendering via `UiPathContext` from
  `flutter_d3_path_port`.
