## 0.1.0

- Initial release: native, fully statically typed pure-Dart port of `d3-contour` v4.0.2
  (`contours`, `Contours`, `ContourMultiPolygon`, `contourDensity`, `contourDensityPoints`,
  `ContourDensity<T>`, `DensityContours`, marching-squares fragment stitching, linear
  interpolation smoothing, signed ring area, polygon containment/hole assignment, and `blur2`
  2D kernel density estimation).
