## 0.1.0

- Initial release: native, fully statically typed pure-Dart port of
  `d3-hierarchy` v3.1.2 (`hierarchy`, `stratify`, `HierarchyNode` traversals
  and transforms, `cluster`, `tree`, `treemap` + all 6 tiling algorithms
  `treemapBinary`, `treemapDice`, `treemapSlice`, `treemapSliceDice`,
  `treemapSquarify`, `treemapResquarify`, `partition`, `pack`, `packSiblings`,
  and `packEnclose`) with zero `dynamic`, zero runtime dependencies, and hot-path
  optimizations for AOT, WebAssembly, and JavaScript.
