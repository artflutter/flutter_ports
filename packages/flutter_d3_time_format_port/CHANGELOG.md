## 0.1.0

- Initial release: native, fully statically typed pure-Dart port of
  `d3-time-format` v4.1.0 (`TimeLocaleDefinition`, `TimeLocale`, `TimeFormat`,
  `TimeParse`, `timeFormatLocale`, `timeFormatDefaultLocale`, `timeFormat`,
  `timeParse`, `utcFormat`, `utcParse`, `isoFormat`, `isoParse`, and all 34
  built-in `const` locale definitions in `timeFormatLocales`) with zero
  `dynamic`, native `DateTime` support, and hot-path optimizations for AOT,
  WebAssembly, and JavaScript.
